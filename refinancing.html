Refinancing

If your loan is more than two years old, chances are you're paying too much. Let's fix that.

Your rate is not set in stone

Lenders reserve their best rates for new customers — which means loyal borrowers quietly pay a premium. We review your current loan for free, negotiate with your existing bank, and if they won't sharpen their pencil, we move you to one of 50+ lenders who will.

How refinancing with us works

  • Free home-loan health check with no obligation
  • Rate renegotiation with your current lender — sometimes no refinance needed
  • Debt consolidation to roll cards and personal loans into one lower repayment
  • Equity release for renovations, investment or a buffer
  • We handle the paperwork; most refinances cost you nothing out of pocket

Every situation is different — the fastest way to find out what's possible is a quick, no-obligation chat. Our advice costs you nothing.
